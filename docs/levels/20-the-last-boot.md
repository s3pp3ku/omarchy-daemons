## What we learned
- It is every earlier level at once: the scout from 12 and 15, the beat from 14, the ice from 9 and 12.
- Both recorded solutions are perfect (60/60); the quota leaves two for mistakes.
- Hatch order matters: the scout is whichever daemon reaches the first window first.
