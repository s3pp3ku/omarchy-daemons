## What we learned
- With no pit in the cache, daemons returning west after the door opens just bounce around the open room: nothing is lost.
- The scout can't be the guard: the first to reach the port is the scout, the next to arrive is the guard.
- Both routes are perfect-ish; the choice is which skills to keep.
