## Identity
- Map size: 560 × 650
- Daemons / quota / time / minimum release rate: 40 / 36 (90%) / 9:00 / 75
- Skills: Blocker 2, Builder 6, Basher 3, Digger 1
- Goal: A zigzag of six floors: four pits, three walls and a metal door. A blocker can guard a pit (it stands a flight behind the builder and is released afterwards), but guards cost blockers and builders, and a crowd released into a pit loses a few. Exact economy: two guards and no spare.
- Puzzle sentence: **Guard → Wall → Guard → Port → Pit → Wall → Pit**

## What we learned
- A guard turns a clump into a stream the builder can stay ahead of: zero deaths at a guarded pit, two or three at an unguarded one.
- Release the guard with a builder: the stairs he lays over the first bridge are walkable, and a second basher is not on offer.
- A dig through the first floor skips both its pit and the wall below, because a digger lands facing east and walls double as turn-arounds.
