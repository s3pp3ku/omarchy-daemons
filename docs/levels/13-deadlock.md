## What we learned
- An explosion clears about 13 px: the doors are 8 px thick so a daemon planted against one clears it.
- Free B first and bridge its pit before A's flood arrives; B runs out as soon as its door opens.
- One-way doors are the cleanest way to force two groups to depend on each other.
