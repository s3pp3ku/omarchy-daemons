## What we learned
- A bridge is walkable in both directions only while it is out; a crowd turned back by a blocker walks away from the dock and back to it, so the bay must be closed on the far side.
- A scout who crosses early can open the next obstacle while the crowd waits.
- Every beat is deterministic: the release is tied to the bridge's phase, not to a stopwatch.
