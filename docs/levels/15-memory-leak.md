## Anti-triviality

## What we learned
- The scout is a single point of failure: if it dies at a press, the quota is gone. Phase the presses so the scout can pass.
- Metal armour under part of a floor keeps a dig from skipping a toll you meant to keep.
- A digger skips one wall at the price of a basher.
