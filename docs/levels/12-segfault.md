## What we learned
- Ice doubles speed, so a press is far less deadly to a stream than to a walker, but a crowd released all at once still needs the beat.
- The first daemon through becomes the scout; the second becomes the gate. Swap them and the scout is held back by his own gate.
- Keep the bay short (a wall next to the hatch) so the tail of the crowd can make the gap.
